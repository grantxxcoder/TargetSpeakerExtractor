LibriMix takes clean audiobook recordings of people reading, and digitally adds two of them together to fake a conversation. It's not a real conversation — nobody interrupted anyone, there's no real room echo, no background clatter. It's clean and artificial (this is what "simulated" means).

AMI is recordings of real meetings — real people talking over each other, real rooms, real microphones. This is what your voice-assistant problem actually looks like.
