Where it stands

M0 is essentially done. Nothing downstream of it has started.

The project has spent its first fortnight on one thing and done it thoroughly: deciding exactly what the training and evaluation data is, and building the code that produces it. Forty-six decisions are logged with their reasoning, every parameter has a config entry, the manifests are rebuilt on corrected measurements, and as of today the renderer turns those manifests into audio.

The renderer was the schedule risk in this milestone — its cost was the one row in the run-time file marked unknown, and the milestone notes braced for an overnight job. Measured: 100 trials in 23.4 seconds, so the full set is about 83 minutes and 27 GB. It is running now.

The honest counterpart has not changed. src/models/, src/eval/ and src/live_model_metric/ are still empty directories. No model exists, and the metric that is the thesis's primary contribution has not been implemented. Those are two separate build efforts and neither has begun.

This fortnight

What B2 changed, and why it mattered

The generator measured how much people talked by how long their audio files were. A LibriSpeech file is only 86 % speech — mostly a third of a second of silence before the reader starts — so every overlap figure in the dataset was overstated. Voice-activity detection now measures from where speech actually is.

Measured on the train split Before After
Both-speaking trials with a wrong overlap label 95.5 % 0.0 %
Worst single-trial label error 0.485 0.0001
True speech overlap in the audio 0.212 0.275
Absence leak — interferer activity AUC 0.648 0.651
Absence leak — speaker identity AUC 0.508 0.503

Unsatisfiable trials, of 20,000 50 62

Re-measured after the rebuild, the correction is worth more than it was on the data that motivated it — the per-trial gap between file-boundary and speech overlap grew from 0.070 to 0.111. Reaching a speech target takes more audio than reaching the same file-duration target, so each trial now carries a longer footprint and file boundaries would misread it by further still.

The mislabelling was not uniform, which is why no correction factor could have fixed it: individual trials were wrong by anywhere up to 0.485, so they sat in the wrong reporting bucket. Since the per-condition breakdown is the thesis's central table, that mattered more than the headline average did.

The audio changed too, not just the labels. Real overlap rose from 0.212 to 0.275 because the placement search now aims at speech, so the generator finally delivers the overlap it was always asking for. The leak scoreboard is unchanged, which is the point — the earlier fixes had to survive this.

Also closed this week

The target activity ceiling came down from 0.85 to 0.78, because 0.85 of speech would need 99 % of the window packed with back-to-back audio and the layout needs gaps. Unreachable draws were being silently dropped. Noise beds with speech in them are now rejected at half a second of unbroken voice — background talkers would otherwise be scored as words the model invented.

Three points the specification left open

Interpreted rather than chosen silently, and each recorded. Noise now covers the reverb tail — A2 predates A5, and read together literally the last stretch would be echo over silence, marking where speech ended. Noise-only trials place the bed where an anchor would have put it, so the condition is not identifiable by loudness alone. The enrollment is levelled, which no decision covers. The EQ curve is also the renderer's own choice; its bands are written into every trial so it stays reportable. All four are worth a supervisor's glance.

Risk

Three things that decide whether this lands

No GPU is secured, and M2 needs 40–70 hours of one

The working machine is a four-core laptop with torch.cuda.is_available() == False. Two M0 items exist specifically to defuse this — surveying public streaming-TSE checkpoints, and resolving HPC access — and neither has been done. The checkpoint survey is described in the milestone file as ten minutes of searching that could save weeks, because a usable causal BSRNN checkpoint turns M2 from a training run into a fine-tune. It is the highest-leverage unstarted task in the project.

The primary contribution is scheduled last

The metric is the deliverable that cannot be cut, and it is finished in week 8 with two milestones downstream of it. Any slip in M0–M2 pushes directly into it. The mitigation is that it must be designed on paper by the end of week 6 even though it is not implemented until week 8 — that drafting has not started either.

Turn-taking is rare in the data, by construction

Searching the 108 two-speaker validation trials for the textbook interruption — target speaking alone, interferer cuts in, target yields, listens, resumes — finds only five. The placement search positions the interferer as one contiguous block to hit an overlap target; it does not model turn-taking, so the pattern occurs by accident. Not a defect, but if interruption handling matters to the argument, that is a data-construction gap rather than something more of the current set will supply.

Closed since the last revision: the renderer's cost, which was the third risk here and the only unknown in the run-time file. Measured at ~83 minutes for the full set.

Open question for the supervisor

C2 — how hard should the task be? Measured as word error rate on the raw mixture, currently targeting a 60–80 % floor. It blocks nothing today, but it cannot be settled without them, and it sets the difficulty of every trial rendered afterwards. Worth resolving before the renderer runs at full scale.
