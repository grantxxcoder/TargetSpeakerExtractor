\subsection{Markov prior}
\label{app:gate-prior}
% Values: experiments/results/2026-10-14-activity-runs.json, sim_tail1 / both / all
% (d_on 596.34, d_off 489.53 frames); the same values are in bsrnn_presence_gate.yaml.
The transition matrix of the Markov prior in \eqref{eq:gate-prior-sequence}, set by \eqref{eq:transition-fit}. Rows are the state in frame $t-1$ and columns the state in frame $t$, with 0 for the target silent and 1 for the target speaking:
\begin{equation}
A =
\begin{bmatrix} A_{00} & A_{01} \\ A_{10} & A_{11} \end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix} 1 - 1/\bar{d}_\textup{off} & 1/\bar{d}_\textup{off} \\ 1/\bar{d}_\textup{on} & 1 - 1/\bar{d}_\textup{on} \end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix} 0.99796 & 0.00204 \\ 0.00168 & 0.99832 \end{bmatrix},
\label{eq:transition-matrix}
\end{equation}
with $\bar{d}_\textup{on} = 596.34$ frames (\SI{4.77}{\second}) and $\bar{d}_\textup{off} = 489.53$ frames (\SI{3.92}{\second}), measured over the \num{9955} training trials with each speaker in turn as the target, as in training.
